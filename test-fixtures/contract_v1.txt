SERVICE AGREEMENT - VERSION 1.0
Effective Date: January 1, 2025

1. PAYMENT TERMS
   Payment must be made within 30 days for domestic orders.
   International orders require payment within 45 days.
   Late payments incur a fee of 5% per month.

2. LIABILITY
   The contractor's liability is capped at $100,000 for normal claims.
   Liability for willful misconduct is unlimited.

3. WARRANTY
   The warranty period is 1 year for the basic license.
   Premium licenses include a 3-year warranty.

4. TERMINATION
   Either party may terminate with 30 days written notice.
   The customer must return all confidential materials upon termination.

5. CONFIDENTIALITY
   All technical documentation is considered confidential.
   The contractor shall not disclose customer data to third parties.

6. DATA RETENTION
   Customer data must be retained for 5 years after termination.
   Personal data shall be deleted within 90 days of a deletion request.
