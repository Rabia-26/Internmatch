SERVICE AGREEMENT - VERSION 2.0
Effective Date: June 1, 2025

1. PAYMENT TERMS
   Payment must be made within 60 days for domestic orders.
   International orders require payment within 45 days.
   Late payments incur a fee of 10% per month.

2. LIABILITY
   The contractor's liability is capped at $1,000,000 for normal claims.
   Liability for willful misconduct is unlimited.

3. WARRANTY
   The warranty period is 2 years for the basic license.
   Premium licenses include a 3-year warranty.

4. TERMINATION
   Either party may terminate with 60 days written notice.
   The customer must not return confidential materials upon termination;
   they are to be securely destroyed instead.

5. CONFIDENTIALITY
   All technical documentation is considered confidential.
   The contractor may disclose anonymized customer data to third parties
   for research purposes.

6. DATA RETENTION
   Customer data must be retained for 5 years after termination.
   Personal data shall be deleted within 30 days of a deletion request.
